I'm a higher education professional, instructor, researcher, and graduate student interested in the intersection of computer science, artificial intelligence, education, and student success.

I currently work at Austin Community College as a Program Coordinator for the Bachelor of Applied Science in Software Development program and as adjunct faculty in Computer Science and Information Technology.

## Currently

- Pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at Texas State University
- Teaching ITNW 1337 - Introduction to the Internet - Web Development at Austin Community College
- Exploring algorithms, artificial intelligence, distributed systems, and software development
- Continuing research related to learning, student experiences, and higher education
