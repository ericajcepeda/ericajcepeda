I'm a higher education professional, instructor, researcher, and graduate student interested in the intersection of computer science, artificial intelligence, education, and student success.

I currently work at Austin Community College District as a Program Coordinator for the Bachelor of Applied Science in Software Development program and as adjunct faculty in Computer Science and Information Technology.

## Currently
- Pursuing an **M.S. in Computer Science** at Texas State University
- Teaching computer science, web development, and digital literacy at Austin Community College District
- Exploring **AI, data mining, software engineering, and programming languages**
- Researching **student learning, higher education, and responsible AI**
- Interested in **human-centered design, educational technology, and student success**
- Bridging my backgrounds in **education, research, and computer science** through interdisciplinary projects
